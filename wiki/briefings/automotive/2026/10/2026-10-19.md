## Question
What new automotive cybersecurity research, incidents, or exploited vulnerabilities cleared the 7-day promotion bar for 2026-10-19?

## Short answer
None clearly did. Direct arXiv searches across automotive security, vehicle security, V2X, CAN, EVSE, and connected-vehicle security found no fresh vehicle-specific security preprint. CISA KEV additions were generic enterprise/security products, and the date-bounded NVD `automotive` query returned zero results. A current search surfaced the Acrisure/KARR Bluetooth disclosure, but its original publication and patch were in July and it was excluded as stale for this run.

## Evidence and decision
- **Academic research:** The date-bounded arXiv screen returned no automotive-security item meeting the relevance bar. The one CAN/security result, *ESAU*, is generic IoT remote attestation rather than automotive research.
- **Advisories:** CISA's 2026-10-12 through 2026-10-19 KEV additions covered BeyondTrust, Fortinet, Atlassian, AhsayCBS, Cisco, PAN-OS, Ivanti, and Microsoft; none is labeled as an automotive, EVSE, telematics, infotainment, or OTA product.
- **CVE validation:** NVD returned `totalResults: 0` for the exact `automotive` keyword/date query. This is a query-bounded negative, not evidence that the sector had no relevant vulnerabilities.
- **Decision:** Keep the existing software-defined-vehicles and automotive-can watchlists active. Continue supplier/product searches for EVSE/OCPP/ISO 15118, telematics/cloud APIs, Android Automotive, CAN/UDS, vehicle Ethernet, OTA infrastructure, and aftermarket devices.
