# Automotive Cybersecurity Briefing — 2026-11-08

**Window:** 2026-11-01 through 2026-11-08 (7-day default; America/New_York)

**Assessment:** Two fresh signals cleared the archive-controlled promotion bar: CANMentor, a privacy-aware cloud-teacher/local-student CAN IDS preprint, and Block Harbor guidance on combining fuzzing breadth with penetration-testing depth. No new automotive-specific CISA KEV/CVE promotion cleared the screen. The AAOS bulletin and GPS-spoofing analysis remain active watchlist repeats.

## Academic research / arXiv

- **Headline:** CANMentor: A Large-Small Model Collaboration Framework for CAN Bus Intrusion Detection
- **Publication date:** November 5, 2026
- **Source family:** Academic preprint / arXiv (primary source)
- **Canonical source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07853
- **Why it matters:** The architecture keeps inference local, sends privacy-aware proxies to a cloud teacher, respects a latency bound, and distills teacher logits into the local model. Reported benchmark gains are promising but simulation/benchmark-bound.
- **Decision note:** Require real-vehicle latency, proxy leakage, cloud-failure, poisoning, and reproducibility tests before treating this as an automotive deployment pattern.
- **Confidence:** Medium — canonical abstract/date verified; preprint claims remain independently unvalidated.

## Threat intelligence / researcher analysis

- **Headline:** Fuzz Testing vs Penetration Testing in Automotive Cybersecurity
- **Publication date:** November 4, 2026
- **Source family:** Automotive cybersecurity practitioner analysis / Block Harbor Insights
- **Canonical source URL:** https://www.blockharbor.io/blog/fuzz-testing-vs-penetration-testing-in-automotive-cybersecurity
- **Why it matters:** The article separates automated interface coverage from human-led attack-path and impact validation, and recommends connecting both to regression evidence across releases.
- **Decision note:** Convert fuzz crashes into pentest hypotheses, confirmed findings into regression tests, and map coverage to TARA priorities with live-system authorization and safety controls.
- **Confidence:** Medium — direct publisher guidance; not an independent vulnerability disclosure.
