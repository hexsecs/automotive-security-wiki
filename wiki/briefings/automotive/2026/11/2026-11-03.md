- **Headline:** CVE-2026-58915 affects Android Automotive OS multi-user passenger displays
- **Publication date:** November 2, 2026
- **Source family:** Android CNA/CVE record and official AAOS bulletin
- **Canonical source URL:** https://source.android.com/docs/security/bulletin/aaos/2026/2026-11-01
