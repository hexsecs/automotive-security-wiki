# Automotive Cybersecurity Briefing — 2026-11-24

**Window:** November 18–24, 2026, through 9:07 a.m. Detroit time.

**Assessment:** Two items from the discovery window are new to the archive. A journal review maps V2X security defenses, and an FIA submission asks WP.29 to consider work on interoperable authentication and authorization for vehicle data.

## Academic research / V2X security

- **Headline:** Giriraj Sharma surveys V2X threats and defenses across DSRC and cellular V2X.
- **Publication date:** November 21, 2026, online. The journal issue is dated 2027.
- **Source family:** Journal review, Peer-to-Peer Networking and Applications.
- **Canonical source:** [Publisher article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12083-026-02312-9)
- **What changed:** The article compares V2X technologies, catalogs proactive and reactive defenses, and discusses data-driven detection.
- **Why it matters:** A V2X threat model can map prevention and detection separately across direct radio links and network-mediated paths, where dependencies and failure modes differ.
- **Decision note:** Read the technology comparison and defense taxonomy to check coverage in a V2X TARA. Evaluate candidate detectors with the evidence checklist in [[concepts/in-vehicle-ids-evaluation|In-Vehicle IDS Evaluation]].
- **Confidence:** High for publication and scope. Treat the review as a map of related work. Deployment suitability requires primary validation.

## Standards and regulation / Vehicle-data access

- **Headline:** FIA asks WP.29 to consider common principles for vehicle-data authentication and authorization.
- **Document date:** November 18, 2026.
- **Source family:** FIA informal document WP.29-200-18, hosted by UNECE.
- **Canonical source:** [WP.29 meeting submission](https://unece.org/sites/default/files/2026-11/WP.29-200-18e.pdf)
- **What changed:** FIA proposed exploring a common reference for roles, assurance levels, and how permissions are expressed and recognized. Its examples include automated-driving records, fuel and energy consumption data, electronic inspections, and digital keys.
- **Why it matters:** An interoperable permission model would need to represent who is asking, what they may access, and how credentials are revoked while accounting for jurisdiction-specific access rights.
- **Decision note:** Review role, grant, and revocation assumptions in [[concepts/connected-vehicle-identity|connected-vehicle identity]] designs. Track the proposal as a watch item.
- **Confidence:** High for the proposal text. This is an informal submission. The session landing page was inaccessible during verification, and a WP.29 decision was not confirmed.
