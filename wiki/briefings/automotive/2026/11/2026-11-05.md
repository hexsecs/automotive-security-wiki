## Threat intelligence / researcher analysis

- **Headline:** An introduction into the Automotive Threat Matrix
- **Publication date:** November 4, 2026
- **Source family:** Automotive cybersecurity practitioner analysis / Block Harbor Insights
- **Canonical source URL:** https://www.blockharbor.io/blog/an-introduction-into-the-automotive-threat-matrix
- **Why it matters:** The article explains the ATM as a shared, vehicle-specific taxonomy of attacker tactics and techniques, maintained through Auto-ISAC industry review. It positions the matrix as a practical prompt list for TARA attack-path discovery, a test-planning aid, and a common language between vehicle security operations and product-security incident response.
- **Decision note:** Add ATM technique IDs to TARA and validation-plan templates, then use the same IDs when handing findings from engineering/test teams to VSOC and PSIRT. Treat this as workflow guidance, not independent evidence that every listed technique is exploitable in a particular vehicle program; verify the live ATM entries and map them to program-specific assets and mitigations.
- **Confidence:** Medium — direct publisher page and publication date verified; the article is practitioner guidance and relies partly on Auto-ISAC materials, not a measured deployment study.
- **Lens:** Automotive cybersecurity; TARA; threat intelligence; incident response
