# Automotive Cybersecurity Briefing — 2026-11-28

**Window:** November 22–28, 2026, through 9:10 a.m. Detroit time.

**Assessment:** A new paper provides a CAN-FD bench measurement method for gateway-to-ECU mutual authentication. Targeted searches of advisories and standards did not surface a verified new item beyond the archive.

## Academic research / In-vehicle authentication

- **Headline:** Wang and colleagues measure a gateway-to-ECU authentication exchange over CAN-FD.
- **Publication date and source family:** November 28, 2026, online. International Journal of Information Security journal article.
- **Canonical source:** [Publisher article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10207-026-01351-w), DOI 10.1007/s10207-026-01351-w.
- **What changed:** The authors propose a challenge-response protocol with SipHash verification and synchronized key updates. On a two-node CAN-FD setup, the authors measured elapsed time through final confirmation and key synchronization, including transport and software processing costs.
- **Why it matters:** End-to-end timing is more informative for an [[concepts/automotive-can|automotive CAN]] startup budget than cryptographic operation timings alone.
- **Decision note:** Read for the measurement setup. Compare a vetted baseline on the target gateway under representative bus load and concurrent ECU startup. Check recovery from interrupted exchanges, key-state desynchronization, and ECU replacement. Require independent cryptographic review before relying on the proposed key update.
- **Confidence:** High for date, design summary, and two-node measurement setup based on publisher excerpts. Direct full-page retrieval was unavailable. Fleet-scale performance, interoperability on other buses, and protocol assurance remain unverified.
