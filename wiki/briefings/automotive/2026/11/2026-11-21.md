- **Headline:** PhantoMasq and the Dataset Observatory remain relevant, but neither is new today

- **Source family:** Academic preprints — arXiv primary sources

- **Why it matters:** PhantoMasq reports graph-based CAN masquerade detection results, while the Dataset Observatory reports limited public evidence for V2X/vehicular-network datasets and widespread processed-only records. - **Confidence:** Medium-high for the source records and stated scope; medium for deployment conclusions because the results remain author-reported and benchmark-bound.
