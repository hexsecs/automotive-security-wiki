# Automotive Cybersecurity Briefing — 2026-11-25

**Window:** November 19–25, 2026, through 8:38 a.m. Detroit time.

**Assessment:** ChargePoint Home Flex firmware triage is the immediate action. A new vehicular network paper and a commercial-vehicle software lifecycle announcement provide research and supplier-evaluation leads.

## Advisories / EV charging

- **Publication date and source family:** November 24, 2026. TrendAI Zero Day Initiative coordinated disclosures.
- **Canonical sources:** [ZDI advisory index](https://www.zerodayinitiative.com/advisories/published/2026/) and [ZDI-26-1113](https://www.zerodayinitiative.com/advisories/ZDI-26-1113/).
- **What changed:** ZDI published six ChargePoint Home Flex advisories, ZDI-26-1110 through ZDI-26-1115, covering CVE-2026-131814 through CVE-2026-131819. ZDI describes CVE-2026-131817 as network-adjacent unauthenticated code execution (CVSS 8.8). The other entries cover authentication bypass or local privilege escalation.
- **Why it matters:** The network-adjacent issue makes reachable Home Flex installations the first priority for [[concepts/ev-charging-security|charging security]] triage.
- **Decision note:** Confirm the installed firmware and the supported fixed release with ChargePoint. ZDI names 2024.02.00.259-alpha for CVE-2026-131817. ChargePoint documents [activation for software updates](https://www.chargepoint.com/resources/activating-your-chargepoint-home). Limit local access from untrusted devices while preserving supported update connectivity.
- **Confidence:** High for the disclosures and product scope. A generally available fixed version for each hardware variant, its rollout status, and in-the-wild exploitation were not verified.

## Academic research / Vehicular ad hoc networks

- **Publication date and source family:** November 23, 2026. Scientific Reports, early publication of an accepted article.
- **Canonical source:** [Publisher article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-75847-9).
- **What changed:** Shobana G and Arockia Xavier Annie R propose a hybrid deep-learning approach to secure clustering for vehicle and roadside communication. The paper motivates the design with message-integrity and confidentiality risks.
- **Why it matters:** Cluster reassignment and trust decisions deserve scrutiny when vehicles move between groups and roadside coverage varies.
- **Decision note:** Treat this as a research lead. Before drawing deployment conclusions, check the threat model, baselines, authentication assumptions, and whether mobility and radio conditions match the intended environment.
- **Confidence:** High for the publication and topic. The publisher landing-page extract was available, but the full methods and results could not be verified.

## Supplier announcement / Commercial-vehicle OTA and diagnostics

- **Publication date and source family:** November 24, 2026. ZF Commercial Vehicle Solutions press release published by Automotive Technology.
- **Canonical source:** [ZF press release](https://www.automotive-technology.com/pressreleases/zfs-digital-toolbox-enables-flexible-pathways).
- **What changed:** ZF introduced Digital Toolbox, a modular offering combining diagnostics, secure ECU access, software management, OTA updates, and component health monitoring. OEMs can adopt services individually or integrate them with their own systems.
- **Why it matters:** Combining these functions makes the division of authority between the OEM backend, supplier services, and vehicle a procurement question.
- **Decision note:** Ask for the authorization and audit boundaries between diagnostics and update approval, including signing-key custody, separation of roles, and recovery when a backend is unavailable.
- **Confidence:** High for the announced scope. The press release does not establish security conformance or field performance.
