## Academic research / arXiv

- **Headline:** A Cross-Domain TARA-to-NAC Rule Mapping Methodology: From Automotive OEM Networks to Energy Storage Systems
- **Publication date:** November 6, 2026
- **Source family:** Academic preprint / arXiv (primary source)
- **Canonical source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09771
- **Why it matters:** The paper proposes a traceable six-step bridge from ISO/SAE 21434 cybersecurity goals to enforceable network access-control rules at named enforcement points. In its desk-based automotive OEM example, 37 threats and 24 goals produce 23 rules covering 11 of 13 in-scope goals, including supplier authorization and legacy OT devices.
- **Decision note:** Use the TARA-to-NAC traceability pattern as a design-review checklist for plant networks and supplier access: every control should point back to a threat record, goal, asset, and enforcement point. Treat the reported coverage as a worked example, not measured operational effectiveness; request production validation, failure-mode analysis, and evidence for rule maintenance before adopting it as a control baseline.
- **Confidence:** Medium — canonical abstract and date verified; the work is a preprint and explicitly says its examples are desk-based.
- **Lens:** Automotive cybersecurity; TARA; OT/network enforcement; supplier security
