# CATARC

## Profile
CATARC (China Automotive Technology and Research Center) runs the public-consultation platform for Chinese automotive standards, including work of the National Automotive Standardization Technical Committee (SAC TC114).

## Contributions in this archive
- **Automotive-AI information security, 10 November 2026:** drafts on AI-specific security requirements and test methods (SAC TC114/SC34), covering AI process assurance, models, cockpit AI and driving-automation applications. ^[sources/catarc-automotive-ai-cybersecurity-guidance-drafts-2026-11-10.md]
- **EV battery-management systems, 10 November 2026:** a recommended national-standard draft that tests legitimate versus unauthorized commands to read or modify BMS memory. ^[sources/catarc-bms-standard-draft-memory-security-2026-11-10.md]

## How to weigh it
Both items are drafts open for comment, not final or mandatory standards. The detailed texts are Chinese-language and should be checked directly before being turned into compliance criteria.
