# VicOne

## Profile
VicOne is an automotive cybersecurity vendor. Its products named in this archive are xCarbon, xZETA and xNexus, and it publishes research through its CyberThreat Research Lab and LAB R7 (physical-AI and robotics security). It is the most frequently cited researcher-analysis source in the archive.

## Contributions in this archive
- **Incident telemetry:** the Q3 2026 situational-awareness report counts 400 incidents, with in-vehicle systems (174) ahead of enterprise IT (149). ^[raw/articles/vicone-q3-2026-automotive-cyber-incidents.md]
- **AI-defined vehicles:** a two-part analysis of how models and agents change vehicle security. ^[raw/articles/vicone-ai-defined-vehicles-2026-10-29.md] ^[raw/articles/vicone-ai-defined-vehicles-securing-decisions-2026-11-19.md]
- **Manipulated safety inputs:** LAB R7 robotics research on safety functions fed false inputs. ^[raw/articles/vicone-lab-r7-manipulated-inputs-2026-11-11.md]
- **GPS spoofing and cargo theft:** with Mobile Defender Inc., a layered detection and response pattern for fleets. ^[raw/articles/vicone-gps-spoofing-cargo-theft-2026-11-04.md]
- **Engineering integration:** a partnership with BlueBinaries to fit VicOne products into OEM and Tier 1 workflows. ^[raw/articles/vicone-bluebinaries-automotive-cybersecurity-workflows-2026-10-27.md]

## How to weigh it
VicOne's incident counts are proprietary observations, its recommendations are vendor guidance, and its product-integration claims are unverified. Keep its recommendations separate from the external research it cites until that research is reviewed directly.
