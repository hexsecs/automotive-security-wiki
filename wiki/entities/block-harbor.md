# Block Harbor

## Profile
Block Harbor is an automotive cybersecurity practitioner that publishes guidance through Block Harbor Insights. Its articles in this archive are workflow guidance rather than vulnerability disclosures.

## Contributions in this archive
- **Automotive Threat Matrix:** an introduction to Auto-ISAC's vehicle-specific taxonomy of attacker tactics and techniques, and how to use its IDs across TARA, testing, threat intelligence and VSOC/PSIRT handoffs. ^[sources/block-harbor-automotive-threat-matrix-2026-11-04.md]
- **Fuzzing versus penetration testing:** automated interface coverage and human-led attack-path validation as complementary, connected by regression evidence across releases. ^[sources/block-harbor-fuzz-vs-pentest-2026-11-04.md]

## How to weigh it
Practitioner guidance, partly relying on Auto-ISAC material. Useful for process design; not evidence that a given technique is exploitable in a given vehicle.
